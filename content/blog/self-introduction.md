Hi, I'm **Yukuan Hao** — a full-stack developer based in Sherbrooke, Quebec, and I'm currently on the hunt for my next role.

## How I got here

My path into software wasn't a straight line. I started out studying **surveying and mapping engineering** at Jilin University, but the more I worked around software, the more I realized the part I actually loved was the code. So I made the jump — a **Master's in Computer Science** at Bishop's University — and I haven't looked back since.

What keeps me hooked is the craft itself. I love understanding how things work under the hood, from network protocols to backend architectures, and then turning that understanding into something people can actually use.

## What I do

I work across the stack — **React, Vue, and Node.js** on the web, plus **Swift and Objective-C** on iOS. At Panda Communications I built and maintained an iOS app and a Vue-based order management system, and along the way I've picked up everything from Supabase and PostgreSQL to Unity and Docker.

## What I'm building now

Lately I've been pouring my energy into personal projects — a Steam achievement "trophy wall," a self-hosted RSS reader, a couple of Unity games, and this very portfolio — both to sharpen my skills and for the sheer fun of building things end to end. You'll find write-ups for all of them right here in this blog.

When I'm not coding, you'll usually find me watching anime, listening to music, or catching a movie.

I'm **actively looking for opportunities** where I can learn from experienced developers and do real, impactful work. If that sounds like a fit, I'd love to hear from you — the quickest way to reach me is the contact section on this site.
